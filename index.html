Flow Sort: Color Water Puzzle – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Flow Sort: Color Water Puzzle ("the game") handles. The game is made by an independent developer, Muzammil Rafiq ("we").

Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not run our own servers.

Game progress

Your levels, stars, coins, themes and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or using "Reset progress" in Settings deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interactions with ads (for example views and taps) and diagnostic data

This data is handled by Google under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive data that identifies you personally.

Your choices

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Security

Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
